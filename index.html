05 FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day. If you'd rather lock in a time, call 0478 554 158.

When are you open?

Monday to Wednesday 9am–5pm, Thursday and Friday 9am–8pm, Saturday 9am–5pm and Sunday 10am–5pm.

Where are you?

58 Palmerston Street, Melton VIC 3337, right in town. Get directions on Google Maps.

What can I get done?

Men's haircuts and skin fades, kids' haircuts, beard trims, head shaves, hot towel shaves, colour, and threading and waxing for brows and facial hair.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Bring them in any day, no booking needed, or call 0478 554 158 to lock in a time.

Is the shop wheelchair accessible?

Yes. There's a wheelchair-accessible entrance, car park, seating and toilet, plus an assistive hearing loop.

How much is a haircut?

Give us a call or drop in and we'll quote you straight away.
